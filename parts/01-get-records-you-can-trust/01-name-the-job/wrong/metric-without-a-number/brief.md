# The job

## Job

Growers email spray requests to the co-op. The desk keys each one into the application system.

## Cost today

Typing every request by hand is slow, and a wrong rate can damage a crop.

## Truth

The jobs the desk keyed by hand, in `truth/dev.jsonl`.

## Metric

Book as many requests as we can, as accurately as we can.

## Never

Never book a job above the product's label rate.
