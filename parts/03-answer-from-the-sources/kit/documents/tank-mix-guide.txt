Title: Tallgrass Bend tank mix guide
--- page 1 ---
Which products mix. Glyfallow 540 mixes with Tillerguard and with Stemsure in one tank.

Pulsepath mixes with Podshield. Brasslock must go on alone, with no other product in the tank.
--- page 2 ---
Order of mixing. Fill the tank half full of water first. Add each product with the agitator running.

Cleaning the tank. Rinse the tank three times after Brasslock or Pulsepath, before the sprayer goes onto another crop.
