Title: Tallgrass Bend crop stage guide
--- page 1 ---
Cereals. Tillerguard goes on wheat and barley from the three-leaf stage. Stemsure goes on before the flag leaf.

Glyfallow 540 never goes on a cereal crop that has come up. Use it before seeding or after harvest.
--- page 2 ---
Pulses and canola. Pulsepath and Podshield go on lentils and peas. Pulsepath goes on before flowering, Podshield at early flowering.

Brasslock goes on canola before bolting. Stemsure also suits canola.
