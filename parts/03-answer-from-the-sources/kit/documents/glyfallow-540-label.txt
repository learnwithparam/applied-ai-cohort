Title: Glyfallow 540 product label
--- page 1 ---
Glyfallow 540 product label. Glyfallow 540 is a herbicide that clears weeds before the crop comes up. Co-op code P100.

Crops and rate. Apply Glyfallow 540 on wheat, barley, canola, lentils and peas. Highest rate: 3.0 L/ha in one season.

Water. Use 100 L of water per hectare with Glyfallow 540.

Timing. Spray before seeding or after harvest, on weeds that are growing well.
--- page 2 ---
Rain. The spray must stay dry on the leaf. Rainfast after 6 hours. Do not spray if rain is likely sooner.

Re-entry. Keep people out of the treated field for 12 hours after spraying.

Harvest. Do not spray within 7 days of harvest.

Buffer zone. Leave 10 metres unsprayed next to water and wetlands.

Storage. Keep the container closed, upright and above 5 degrees. Never reuse it.
