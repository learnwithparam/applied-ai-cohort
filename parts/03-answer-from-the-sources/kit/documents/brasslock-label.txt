Title: Brasslock product label
--- page 1 ---
Brasslock product label. Brasslock is a herbicide for grass weeds in canola. Co-op code P110.

Crops and rate. Apply Brasslock on canola. Highest rate: 1.0 L/ha in one season.

Water. Use 80 L of water per hectare with Brasslock.

Timing. Spray from the two-leaf stage until the canola starts to bolt.
--- page 2 ---
Rain. The spray must stay dry on the leaf. Rainfast after 1 hour. Do not spray if rain is likely sooner.

Re-entry. Keep people out of the treated field for 24 hours after spraying.

Harvest. Do not spray within 60 days of harvest.

Buffer zone. Leave 5 metres unsprayed next to water and wetlands.

Storage. Keep the container closed, upright and above 5 degrees. Never reuse it.
