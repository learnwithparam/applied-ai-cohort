Title: Stemsure product label
--- page 1 ---
Stemsure product label. Stemsure is a growth regulator that keeps stems short and strong. Co-op code P140.

Crops and rate. Apply Stemsure on wheat, barley and canola. Highest rate: 0.75 L/ha in one season.

Water. Use 90 L of water per hectare with Stemsure.

Timing. Spray once, before the crop reaches the flag leaf stage.
--- page 2 ---
Rain. The spray must stay dry on the leaf. Rainfast after 3 hours. Do not spray if rain is likely sooner.

Re-entry. Keep people out of the treated field for 36 hours after spraying.

Harvest. Do not spray within 50 days of harvest.

Buffer zone. Leave 3 metres unsprayed next to water and wetlands.

Storage. Keep the container closed, upright and above 5 degrees. Never reuse it.
