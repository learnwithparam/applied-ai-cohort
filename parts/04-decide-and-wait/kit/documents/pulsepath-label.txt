Title: Pulsepath product label
--- page 1 ---
Pulsepath product label. Pulsepath is a herbicide for broadleaf weeds in pulse crops. Co-op code P130.

Crops and rate. Apply Pulsepath on lentils and peas. Highest rate: 1.2 L/ha in one season.

Water. Use 60 L of water per hectare with Pulsepath.

Timing. Spray when the weeds are small and before the crop flowers.
--- page 2 ---
Rain. The spray must stay dry on the leaf. Rainfast after 4 hours. Do not spray if rain is likely sooner.

Re-entry. Keep people out of the treated field for 48 hours after spraying.

Harvest. Do not spray within 30 days of harvest.

Buffer zone. Leave 20 metres unsprayed next to water and wetlands.

Storage. Keep the container closed, upright and above 5 degrees. Never reuse it.
