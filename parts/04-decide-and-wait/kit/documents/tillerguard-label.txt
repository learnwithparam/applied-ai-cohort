Title: Tillerguard product label
--- page 1 ---
Tillerguard product label. Tillerguard is a fungicide for leaf disease in cereals. Co-op code P120.

Crops and rate. Apply Tillerguard on wheat and barley. Highest rate: 0.8 L/ha in one season.

Water. Use 120 L of water per hectare with Tillerguard.

Timing. Spray at the first sign of disease, from the three-leaf stage.
--- page 2 ---
Rain. The spray must stay dry on the leaf. Rainfast after 2 hours. Do not spray if rain is likely sooner.

Re-entry. Keep people out of the treated field for 18 hours after spraying.

Harvest. Do not spray within 45 days of harvest.

Buffer zone. Leave 15 metres unsprayed next to water and wetlands.

Storage. Keep the container closed, upright and above 5 degrees. Never reuse it.
