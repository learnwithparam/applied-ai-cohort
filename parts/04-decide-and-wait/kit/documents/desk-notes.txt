Title: Spray desk notes on common questions
--- page 1 ---
Glyfallow 540 and Brasslock questions. Growers often ask how long Glyfallow 540 and Brasslock must stay dry, how close to harvest they can go on, and the buffer zone near water. Read the second page of each label.

Growers also ask when people can walk back into a field after Glyfallow 540 or Brasslock, and how much water each needs per hectare.
--- page 2 ---
Tillerguard and Stemsure questions. Growers often ask how long Tillerguard and Stemsure must stay dry after spraying, the buffer zone near a creek, and how many days before harvest the last spray can be.

Growers also ask when people can go back into a field after Tillerguard or Stemsure, and the highest rate on wheat.
--- page 3 ---
Pulsepath and Podshield questions. Growers often ask how long Pulsepath and Podshield must stay dry, how close to harvest they can go on, and the buffer zone near a wetland or water.

Growers also ask when people can walk into a field sprayed with Pulsepath or Podshield, and how much water per hectare to use.
