Title: Tallgrass Bend spray guide
--- page 1 ---
Rates per acre and per hectare. One hectare is 2.471 acres. Some growers write rates per acre.

To turn L per acre into L/ha, multiply by 2.471. For example, 0.97 L per acre is 2.4 L/ha. The desk always books rates in L/ha.
--- page 2 ---
Wind and drift. Do not spray when the wind is above 15 km/h. Fine drops drift onto the next field.

Do not spray when the wind is below 3 km/h either. Still air can hold a cloud of spray that moves later. Brasslock and Glyfallow 540 harm canola and pulses next door if they drift.
--- page 3 ---
Rain and dew. Rain soon after spraying washes product off the leaf. Check the rainfast time on each label before you spray.

Heavy dew can also dilute a spray. Glyfallow 540 and Pulsepath need a longer dry spell than Podshield. Tillerguard and Stemsure fall in between.
