Title: Podshield product label
--- page 1 ---
Podshield product label. Podshield is a fungicide that protects pods from disease. Co-op code P150.

Crops and rate. Apply Podshield on lentils and peas. Highest rate: 0.5 L/ha in one season.

Water. Use 150 L of water per hectare with Podshield.

Timing. Spray at early flowering, and again 14 days later if the weather stays wet.
--- page 2 ---
Rain. The spray must stay dry on the leaf. Rainfast after 30 minutes. Do not spray if rain is likely sooner.

Re-entry. Keep people out of the treated field for 8 hours after spraying.

Harvest. Do not spray within 21 days of harvest.

Buffer zone. Leave 25 metres unsprayed next to water and wetlands.

Storage. Keep the container closed, upright and above 5 degrees. Never reuse it.
